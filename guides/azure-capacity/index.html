01 / Capacity fundamentals

Permission
is not placement.

Three concepts. Three different questions. One deployment depends on all of them.

01

Quota

"How much may my subscription deploy?"

A scoped limit
02

Capacity

"Can the infrastructure meet this exact request?"

SKU, region, zone, constraints
03

Allocation

"Can Azure place the resources when I deploy?"

A deployment-time outcome
A product can be offered in a region and your quota can be approved, while the requested allocation still fails. Availability is not a promise of capacity.

01 / Capacity for AI workloads

Not all capacity
speaks vCPUs.

Capture model/version, deployment type, region, workload shape, and processing-location requirements before you size.

Deployment location alone does not establish where inference is processed. Verify current data-processing rules for the exact model and deployment type.

TPM
RPM
MODEL
Unused PTU quota does not guarantee deployable model capacity. For provisioned throughput, create the deployment before purchasing matching billing-reservation coverage.

02 / Start a better conversation

Constraints aren't
the end of the story.
They're design inputs.

Ask what must remain fixed, what can change, and what evidence is still missing. Never trade compliance for convenience.

Place

Approved regions, zones, residency, latency, dependencies.

Shape

Compatible SKU, model/version, features, performance.

Pace

Initial demand, peak demand, launch date, growth ramp.

Protection

Reliability targets, failover readiness, supported reservations.

A useful commitment: "Let's validate the exact configuration and agree a compliant fallback, an owner, and the next checkpoint."

03 / Public processes, tools & resources

Resolve
the right problem.

Choose the evidence you have. A quota request, allocation investigation, and growth plan are not interchangeable.

01
Classify

Exact service, configuration, scope, quota/usage, and error.

02
Prepare

Demand with units, impact, dates, constraints, alternatives.

03
Request and track

Use the supported public portal route. Record the request ID.

04
Revalidate

Agree ownership and recheck before committing or deploying.

Permissions, subscription eligibility, and support-plan requirements apply. Approval and support response times vary; neither a submitted ticket nor a quota increase guarantees allocation.

03 / From concern to a precise request

Clarity is
a force multiplier.

Replace "we need more capacity" with a request someone can investigate.

"We need [total demand + units] for [exact configuration] in [region/zone] by [date]. Our current quota/usage is [values]. Here is the error, impact, and the alternatives we can accept."

Use your authorized Azure support channel for resource identifiers and diagnostic evidence. Do not put credentials, secrets, or unnecessary personal information in requests.

ScopeSubscription, service, SKU or model/version, deployment type, region/zone.
DemandCurrent usage, approved limits, additional demand, requested total, peak and dates.
EvidenceExact error, timestamp, correlation ID, relevant configuration, prior request ID.
ChoicesHard requirements, allowed alternatives, technical trade-offs and impact.
Follow-upAccountable owner, next update, and revalidation checkpoint.
